1. **MAJOR — The single-box design changes clipping for every stroked rectangle, not only rounded ones.** The proposal claims existing templates remain identical and identifies rounded-corner clipping as the sole reversal ([proposal.md:17](</home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-287/openspec/changes/issue-287-container-geometry/proposal.md:17>), [proposal.md:33](</home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-287/openspec/changes/issue-287-container-geometry/proposal.md:33>)). The design moves the stroke onto the child-holding, clipping box ([design.md:108](</home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-287/openspec/changes/issue-287-container-geometry/design.md:108>)). Typst passes that box’s stroke into `clip_rect` ([inline/box.rs:64](</home/pfa/.cargo/registry/src/index.crates.io-1949cf8c6b5b557f/typst-layout-0.15.1/src/inline/box.rs:64>)), which incorporates half the stroke width and constructs the clip from the stroke’s inner control points ([shapes.rs:626](</home/pfa/.cargo/registry/src/index.crates.io-1949cf8c6b5b557f/typst-layout-0.15.1/src/shapes.rs:626>), [shapes.rs:638](</home/pfa/.cargo/registry/src/index.crates.io-1949cf8c6b5b557f/typst-layout-0.15.1/src/shapes.rs:638>)). Today the child box has no stroke and clips at the outer box ([src/render/mod.rs:2144](</home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-287/src/render/mod.rs:2144>)). Consequently, even an unrounded stroked container will newly cut child ink from the inner half-stroke band instead of allowing it to draw over the stroke. The plan must either redesign emission to preserve outer-box clipping or explicitly accept, specify, and test this additional behavior change; its present compatibility and “same boundary” claims are false.

Load-authoritativeness must be classified and exposed by `resolver.rs`—for example as provenance on `AxisSpec` or a resolver helper—so validation branches only on resolver output.

3. **MAJOR — “Square” has no defined numeric comparison, making the central refusal contract ambiguous.** The delta exhaustively defines when squareness is checked but never defines whether resolved widths and heights must be bit-equal, within an epsilon, or equal after emitter quantization ([spec.md:40](</home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-287/openspec/changes/issue-287-container-geometry/specs/shape-paint/spec.md:40>), [spec.md:53](</home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-287/openspec/changes/issue-287-container-geometry/specs/shape-paint/spec.md:53>)). This matters because resolution uses `f32`, constant `to` extents involve subtraction, existing geometry checks use a `1e-4` tolerance ([src/resolver.rs:289](</home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-287/src/resolver.rs:289>)), and emitted lengths are rounded to four decimals ([src/render/helpers.rs:285](</home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-287/src/render/helpers.rs:285>)). For example, a width calculated as `0.3 - 0.2` and a literal height `0.1` can paint identically yet fail exact floating-point equality. The contract and design must choose the comparison rule and cover a near-equal/differently-derived pair.
