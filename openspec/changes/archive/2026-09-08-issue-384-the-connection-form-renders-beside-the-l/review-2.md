1. **[P1] Default-setting writes bypass the proposed pending-write protection.** [design.md:73](/home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-384/openspec/changes/issue-384-the-connection-form-renders-beside-the-l/design.md:73) retains the shared setting hooks and puts eviction at the control’s call site, while [proposal.md:91](/home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-384/openspec/changes/issue-384-the-connection-form-renders-beside-the-l/proposal.md:91) assigns mutation keys only to connection save/delete. The retained hooks have no mutation key and only invalidate settings ([queries.ts:208](/home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-384/ui/src/api/queries.ts:208), [queries.ts:221](/home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-384/ui/src/api/queries.ts:221)). Consequently, choosing a default and immediately opening Connect can latch the old default, violating the explicit [pending-setting scenario at spec.md:223](/home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-384/openspec/changes/issue-384-the-connection-form-renders-beside-the-l/specs/default-connection/spec.md:223). Eviction in a `mutate` call-site callback also disappears upon unmount, according to [TanStack’s callback documentation](https://tanstack.com/query/latest/docs/framework/react/guides/mutations#mutation-side-effects). The design needs keyed default-write/reset mutations with cache maintenance that survives unmount.

2. **[P1] Cache maintenance and navigation require different lifetimes.** [design.md:81](/home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-384/openspec/changes/issue-384-the-connection-form-renders-beside-the-l/design.md:81) puts eviction and navigation in the same success handler, while [connections/spec.md:229](/home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-384/openspec/changes/issue-384-the-connection-form-renders-beside-the-l/specs/connections/spec.md:229) unconditionally sends successful deletes to `/connections`. But its [pending-delete scenario at line 330](/home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-384/openspec/changes/issue-384-the-connection-form-renders-beside-the-l/specs/connections/spec.md:330) requires an operator who already navigated to Connect to proceed there after deletion. A durable combined handler redirects that operator away; a component-scoped combined callback loses eviction upon unmount. Separate durable cache maintenance from navigation, and explicitly limit completion navigation to the initiating form still being active.

3. **[P2] Several return scenarios assume the connection or resource is restored.** [default-connection/spec.md:112](/home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-384/openspec/changes/issue-384-the-connection-form-renders-beside-the-l/specs/default-connection/spec.md:112) explicitly discards the hand-picked connection and browsing context. Nevertheless, [connections/spec.md:302](/home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-384/openspec/changes/issue-384-the-connection-form-renders-beside-the-l/specs/connections/spec.md:302) requires returning after an edit to browse the edited upstream and re-request that connection’s schema. With default A and previously hand-picked B, returning correctly selects A, making those scenarios false. Similarly, the [transform scenarios at spec.md:31](/home/pfa/projects/labeler/.worktrees/issue-384/openspec/changes/issue-384-the-connection-form-renders-beside-the-l/specs/connector-field-transforms/spec.md:31) promise derived columns or values without consistently requiring selection of the edited connection and affected resource. Add those preconditions and preserve the stated fresh-resolution behavior.
