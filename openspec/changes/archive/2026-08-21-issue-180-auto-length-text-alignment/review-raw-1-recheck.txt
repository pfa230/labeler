    66	/// Every entry of `dir`, sorted. Loading in sorted order rather than `read_dir` order is what makes
    67	/// the winner of an id collision a property of the directory contents instead of a property of this
    68	/// filesystem's enumeration (#181). Every path shares the same parent, so ordering by path is
    69	/// ordering by filename.
    70	fn sorted_dir_paths(dir: &FsPath) -> Result<Vec<PathBuf>, TemplateRegistryError> {
    71	    let entries = std::fs::read_dir(dir).map_err(|source| TemplateRegistryError::Io {
    72	        path: dir.to_path_buf(),
    73	        source,
    74	    })?;
    75	    let mut paths = Vec::new();
    76	    for entry in entries {
    77	        let entry = entry.map_err(|source| TemplateRegistryError::Io {
    78	            path: dir.to_path_buf(),
    79	            source,
    80	        })?;
    81	        paths.push(entry.path());
    82	    }
    83	    paths.sort();
    84	    Ok(paths)
    85	}

     7	## ADDED Requirements
     8	
     9	### Requirement: Auto-width text measures to its content and renders into its alignment slot
    10	
    11	On a dynamic-width `single` template (`format.width: { min, max }`), a `text` item whose `size` width
    12	component is `auto` resolves its width in two independent places, and they SHALL NOT be required to
    13	agree:
    14	
    15	- **Measurement.** The pre-render pass SHALL fit the text within a budget of
    16	  `min(max_w, frame_remainder)`, where `frame_remainder` is the enclosing frame's width (`width.max`
    17	  at the top level, the container's padded inner width inside a container) less the item's resolved
    18	  `at.x`, and SHALL contribute `at.x + fitted_width` to the label's content extent. The label width
    19	  SHALL remain `content_extent` clamped to `[width.min, width.max]`. This is unchanged by this
    20	  requirement: what the label shrinks to is always the fitted content, never the alignment slot
    21	  below.

    25	    slot is `min(max_w, frame_remainder)` measured against the **final** label width, and never

     2	
     3	See proposal.md - Why for the defect and specs/auto-length-layout/spec.md for the contract.
     4	
     5	The constraint that shapes everything here is that the auto-length pipeline runs **twice** over the
     6	same layout, and the two passes must agree on which items they touch:
     7	
     8	- `RenderContext::measure` (`src/render/mod.rs:916`) walks the tree against a frame of `width.max`,
     9	  fits every frame-dependent `text` into `min(max_w, budget_remainder)`, pushes a `MeasuredText`,
    10	  and returns the content extent. `render_single_label` clamps that extent into
    11	  `[width.min, width.max]` and that becomes the page width (`src/render/mod.rs:342`).
    12	- The render pass then walks the same tree against a frame of the **final** width, and
    13	  `render_text_item` replays the measured fit by consuming `MeasuredText` entries through a shared
    14	  cursor (`src/render/mod.rs:1364-1376`). Both passes gate on the same
    15	  `Placement::width_is_frame_dependent()` plus the same right-anchor skip, and a mismatch is a hard
    16	  `AutoLengthCursorMismatch` error, so the *set* of items on this branch must not change.
    17

    22	  size. Only where the glyphs sit inside the box can change.

    24	  this branch is unreachable under rotation and no rotation interaction exists.

    56	
    57	### 1. Widen the render box, never the measured width

    64	auto-length label render at `width.max` - it would never shrink to its text again, which is the
    65	entire point of an auto-length tape. So the two numbers must be allowed to differ: the measured
    66	width says *how wide the label should be*, the rendered box says *how much room the glyphs may be
    67	aligned within*. That split is the ADR.

   577	right.
   578	
   579	A coordinate component may be **edge-relative**: a *sign-negative* value is measured inward from the
   580	frame's far edge rather than outward from its origin.
   581	
   582	| Component | Sign | Measured from |
   583	| --- | --- | --- |
   584	| `x` | non-negative | left edge |
   585	| `x` | sign-negative | **right** edge: `frame_width + x` |
   586	| `y` | non-negative | bottom edge |
   587	| `y` | sign-negative | **top** edge: `frame_height + y` |
   588
