1. **MAJOR — The proposed specs require opposite thumbnail behavior for an unresolvable declared default.** `specs/param-resolution/spec.md:193-196`, `:216-224`, and `:270-275` require the thumbnail to resolve the default and fail. Conversely, `specs/template-inputs/spec.md:534-540` and `:726-732` require it to substitute a placeholder and render. Current behavior follows the latter: `src/templates.rs:433-438` marks a failed default as required, and `src/templates.rs:171-194` supplies placeholders for required interpolated inputs. Both requirements are being modified, so this contradiction would become proposed normative truth and leaves implementation unverifiable. Changing current behavior is also outside #213 and conflicts with `openspec/config.yaml:117-119`.

2. **MAJOR — The first-touch `when:` contract accidentally changes explicit-null behavior while claiming to preserve existing behavior.** `specs/conditional-visibility/spec.md:21-26` says any written `when:` key must contain at least one condition. Elsewhere the plan explicitly defines “written” to include YAML null (`specs/list-params/spec.md:19-22`). Therefore this wording refuses `when: null`. Today null deserializes to `None` (`src/raw.rs:117-145`, `:224-225`) and bypasses validation (`src/templates.rs:2240-2251`), while the delta’s supersession note says these shape rules are unchanged (`specs/conditional-visibility/spec.md:5-13`) and the design repeats that claim (`design.md:238-246`). The complete first-touch contract must explicitly preserve `when: null` as absence, or scope and design the new refusal; it cannot assert both.
